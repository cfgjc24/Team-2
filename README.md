## Project Description:
We have built a web app that allows students to complete their necessary enrollment forms, while also keeping up with school and maintaining communication with tutors. Tutoring is a key component of the app, as it allows students to get the help they need to succeed in their classes. The app also includes a progress feature that allows students to keep track of their assignments. Finally, we have implemented pre session and post session surveys that show the impact of each lesson and help us improve our tutoring services, while also proving to external donors that the product is making a difference in students' lives, gaining more support.

## Components:
- Login page that asks for the role of the user. It then takes you to the proper portal based on the role selected: (Student, Tutor, Admin).
- Student Portal: 
  - Student can view their assignments and progress.
  - Student can view their tutor's information.
  - Student can complete enrollment forms.
  - Student can complete pre and post session surveys.
- Tutor Portal:
    - Tutor can view their students' information.
    - Tutor can take attendance for their students.
    - Tutor can view their students' pre and post session surveys.
- Admin Portal:
    - Admin can view all students' information.
    - Admin can view all students' pre and post session surveys.
    - Admin can view all students' assignments and progress.
    - Admin can view graphs of the data collected based on students success, and also being able to export these as CSV files to send to external donors to show the impact of the tutoring services.

## Tools:

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Vite, Material-UI

Backend: Node.js, Firebase, AG Grit (CSV reports), Chart.js

## Accessibility: 
- The app is designed to be accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. It is a highly contrast theme with large text and buttons. It is also screen reader friendly while also prioritizing the use of FGI theme colors.
