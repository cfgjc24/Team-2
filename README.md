## Project Description:
We have built a web app that allows students to complete their necessary enrollment forms, while also keeping up with school and maintaining communication with tutors. Tutoring is a key component of the app, as it allows students to get the help they need to succeed in their classes. The app also includes a progress feature that allows students to keep track of their assignments. Finally, we have implemented pre session and post session surveys that show the impact of each lesson and help us improve our tutoring services, while also proving to external donors that the product is making a difference in students' lives, gaining more support.
